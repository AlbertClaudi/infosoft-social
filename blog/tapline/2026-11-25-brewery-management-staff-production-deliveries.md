# Brewery Management Software: Bringing Staff, Production and Deliveries Into One Place

Running a craft brewery means wearing many hats. In one day you might plan a brew, sort out who is working the taproom, and make sure kegs reach the right customers. When each of those lives in a different spreadsheet, chat thread or notebook, the admin starts eating into brewing time.

## Three areas that pull owners in different directions

### 1. Staff
Who is working, when, and on what. Without one schedule everyone can see, gaps and double-bookings happen.

### 2. Production
What is brewing, what is ready, and what needs to happen next. Notes scattered across paper and phones make it hard to see the full picture.

### 3. Deliveries
Which orders are going out, where, and when. A missed delivery is a missed sale and an unhappy customer.

## Why one platform beats a stack of tools

- **One source of truth**: staff, production and deliveries in the same place.
- **Less switching**: no jumping between spreadsheets and apps.
- **Clearer handovers**: the next shift sees what the last one did.
- **More time for beer**: less admin means more time in the brewhouse and the taproom.

## A simple way to start

1. List your regular staff and shifts.
2. Add what is currently in production.
3. Enter this week's deliveries.
4. Run the week from the platform and see what you no longer need on paper.
