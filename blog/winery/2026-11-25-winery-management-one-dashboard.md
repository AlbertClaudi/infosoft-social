# Why Wineries Are Moving to One Dashboard (and How to Try It Free for 90 Days)

A winery is many businesses in one: growing, making, selling and hosting guests. Each part often gets its own tool, and the owner ends up stitching them together by hand. The result is time spent on admin instead of on the wine and the people who drink it.

## Signs your winery has outgrown its tools

- You copy the same information between spreadsheets.
- Different people keep different versions of the "real" numbers.
- Answering a simple question means opening several apps.
- New staff need a long list of logins and workarounds.

## What "one dashboard" changes

- **Everything in one place**: the team works from the same information.
- **Fewer handoffs**: less copying between tools means fewer mistakes.
- **Faster answers**: see what is happening across the winery at a glance.
- **Easier onboarding**: one platform to learn instead of many.

## How to test a new platform without risk

1. **Pick one area to start**, where today's process hurts most.
2. **Run it in parallel** for a few weeks next to your current method.
3. **Bring the team in early** and listen to what slows them down.
4. **Use the whole trial**: a long trial lets you see a full cycle of work before you pay.
