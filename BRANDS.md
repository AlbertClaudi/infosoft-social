## Format (since 2026-11-26)
Every brand post is a **12-second vertical video (1080x1920) with light background music**, made with tools/make_reel.py
(headlines/features in POSTS inside tools/gen_illustrated.py; illustration SVGs in ILLUS; music is original, generated by tools/music.py).
The same video is published twice:
- as a **Reel** (instagram type reel, shouldShareToFeed true, caption + hashtags, thumbnailOffset 9000) at 10:00 / 10:05 / 10:10
- as a **Story** (instagram type story, shouldShareToFeed false, no caption) at 10:30 / 10:35 / 10:40
All automated posts carry the Buffer tag **autopost** (id 6b0720ea08b04d1b69799024). Use only that tag to decide the rotation, so manual posts by Albert are ignored.
Buffer free plan allows 10 scheduled posts at once: one day of group A = 6, group B = 4.
